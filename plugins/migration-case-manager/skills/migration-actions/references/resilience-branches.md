# Family and resilience branches

Create a branch with `migration-os branch create <case-directory> <dependent|school|pet|lost_document|urgent_abroad> --owner <person-id>` as soon as the case includes an infant, dependant, school-age child, pet, an urgent loss, or a person already abroad. A branch is an explicit work queue; it does not establish a legal right or replace emergency services.

- `dependent`: record the dependant's route, travel-document, care, and family-evidence questions; source-link any immigration requirement.
- `school`: identify age, city, language, timetable, and enrollment authority before researching options. Do not claim eligibility from a school website.
- `pet`: identify species, route, carrier, microchip/vaccination evidence, and destination authority; keep veterinary evidence outside Markdown.
- `lost_document`: record the document category and location only at a high level. Stop for identity verification, consular declarations, police reports, or signatures; these are human-only.
- `urgent_abroad`: prioritize immediate safety, lawful stay, accommodation, communications, medical/emergency handoff, and consular contact. Never delay emergency services while researching.

Every external submission, appointment, payment, identity check, or disclosure still requires the normal `ACT-*` class and current scoped `DEC-*` decision.
