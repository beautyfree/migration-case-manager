# Source policy

Treat an official source as a government agency, embassy/consulate, a government-designated application provider, or the issuing authority for the document. Record an official page even when its content is delivered as a PDF or online form.

Do not treat a law-firm article, social post, travel blog, search snippet, forum, or commercial visa service as proof. These may identify a question that must be verified elsewhere.

If the destination authority and consulate disagree, preserve both records and mark the requirement `blocked`; do not decide based on search ranking. If the destination has a country-specific portal, prefer its route-specific result over a general guide.
